# PACT Reinforcing Exercise Suite — v0.3

**Development draft. Not approved, accredited, or authorized for live-case actions.**

## What is included
- 11 separate editable student exercise packets: E00, E01, E02, EPROV (M03), E03, E04, E05, E06 (M07), E07, E08, P05.
- Consolidated editable student workbook and staged synthetic source dossier.
- Controlled Instructor Master with timing, release rules, alternative interpretations, exemplars and provisional scoring anchors.
- Instructor-only *development* K01/K02 items (not a secure validated examination).
- Staged CSV/TXT datasets and a separate objective-linked tracking workbook (generated separately).

## Presentation alignment
Deck IDs follow the existing v0.2 decks. EPROV maps to the M03 provenance handoff; E06 is the M07 range exercise. The original deck labels and assessment IDs P01–P05 / K01–K02 are preserved.

## Delivery rule
Release only the daily and exercise-specific records, not the entire source dossier or instructor master at once. Students first produce independent work; then rehearse cross-role handoff. Scores require approved student-performance standards after SME validation and assessor calibration.

## Case constraints
Northbank Regional Medical, BRKR_AL, domains, indicators and persons are fictional. All IPs are documentation addresses. Do not execute commands against any endpoint, contact an actual provider, generate legal process, or perform unsupervised forensic acquisition.
