## Faculty checks before release
1. Confirm intended student population, actual duties, prerequisites and assessment pathway.
2. Confirm counsel/privacy information sharing guidance, source-label approvals, and instructor range authorization.
3. Reconcile exercises' time allocations with the approved program of instruction.
4. Verify source data, test items, criterion rubrics, critical errors and remediation with operational and assessment SMEs.
5. Pilot with mixed investigators and analysts, establish panel throughput and individual evidence retention, and document revisions.
