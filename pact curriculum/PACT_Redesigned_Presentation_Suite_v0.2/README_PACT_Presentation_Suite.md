# PACT | Redesigned Presentation Suite v0.2

**Status:** Proposed development package for agency SME, legal, privacy, assessment, range, and accreditation review. Not an approved/accredited program.

## Source basis
The source package was built from the October 26–30, 2026 agenda; the OneDrive archive (8 presentations and 7 lesson plans); PACT Curriculum Architecture v0.1; and the companion task/objective/assessment matrix.

## Design rules
- The same fictional Case HARBORLINE runs throughout the five-day course. All records, vendors, domains, IP addresses and incident events in that case are synthetic training material.
- Each substantive presentation states measurable objectives and contains investigator-specific and analyst-specific work products.
- Practical exercises use individual written artifacts for performance evidence, with group handoff practice as a separate collaborative activity.
- Instructor notes are embedded on every slide; where a complete instructor lesson plan or exercise packet remains unapproved, those notes provide facilitation prompts but do not constitute an approved lesson plan.
- The materials deliberately do not confer legal process, digital forensic acquisition, digital asset seizure, intelligence release, or tool use authority.

## Version updates and review notes
1. Original Threat Landscape materials referred to 14 Enterprise ATT&CK tactics. The redesigned module references MITRE Enterprise v20 (October 2026), which lists 15. Confirm currency again before delivery.
2. Original Investigator Toolbox materials used TLP:WHITE. Current FIRST TLP v2.0 uses TLP:CLEAR and recognizes AMBER+STRICT.
3. The Legal Frameworks deck used universal process rules and compressed the constitutional/statutory distinction. Replaced with decision points, source and scope specificity, and explicit counsel-review triggers.
4. The archive presented nonuniform lecture durations and International Evidence/Toolbox schedule placement. This suite follows the draft v0.1 module architecture rather than claiming to be the approved October POI.
5. The individual checkpoints shown in decks are *examples*. K01/K02 secure item banks, practical rubrics, mastery rules and standards-setting require approval.
6. The cyber range deck is a controlled facilitation and observation brief, not an operational penetration-testing manual or independent lab authorization.
7. The Friday panel throughput model is provisional and requires a pilot with the actual audience size, number of evaluators and assessment requirements.

## Faculty review gates
- G0: program sponsor, audience and accreditation pathway approval.
- G1: investigator and analyst job-task validation.
- G2: approved objective-to-instruction-to-assessment crosswalk and POI timing.
- G3: complete lesson plans, scenario packets, answer keys, secure written examinations and calibrated rubrics.
- G4: pilot with both pathways and record corrections.
- G5: formal authorized release, configuration control and course evaluations.
